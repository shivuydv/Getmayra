# Release Notes - v1.0.0 (Initial Public Release)

We are excited to launch the first public version of **MYRA**, the ultimate AI companion for Android.

### What's New
- **AI Core**: Full support for Gemini 1.5 Flash with Hinglish/Hindi conversation capabilities.
- **Visuals**: Animated Anime Girl visualizer with blinking and hair sway.
- **HUD Mode**: Maya Cyber HUD for a futuristic terminal-like interface.
- **Automations**: Voice-triggered flashlight, battery status, and app launchers.
- **WhatsApp Integration**: Maya can now read and notify you of new WhatsApp messages in real-time.
- **Home Screen Presence**: Interactive Live Wallpaper service included.
- **Security**: Maya Guardian biometric call screening simulation.

### Known Limitations
- Background listening requires the foreground notification to stay active.
- ElevenLabs voice may have latency depending on network speed.
- QR Scanner requires Google Play Services to be updated.
